Christmas Packaging Order Checklist: Dates, Samples and Artwork

Plan Christmas packaging from the arrival date back. Compare artwork changes, inserts, sample approvals and quotation details without assumed lead times.

MXPACKPRO team · Published 2026-10-30

Before ordering Christmas packaging, confirm when empty packs must reach your filling location, freeze the contents and quantity per design, and approve a packed sample. Work backwards through filling, inbound delivery, production and approvals using dates confirmed for your project. A supplier's production estimate alone is not a Christmas arrival commitment. Start with this checklist before comparing quotations.

Start with the packaging-in-hand date, not December 25

Write down the date empty packaging must be at the bakery, warehouse or packing partner. This can be earlier than the date a customer receives the gift: you still need to receive the cartons, check them, fill the packs and dispatch the finished orders. A countdown product also needs a different launch plan from a Christmas Day gift.

Use one location, postcode and time zone on the brief. Ask who is responsible for each stage and whether an estimate is in working days or calendar days. Record closure dates, approval cutoffs and a buffer you have agreed with the relevant partner. Unknown stages stay marked unconfirmed; do not substitute another supplier's published lead time.

Separate a festive artwork change from a new structure

A familiar box with a new illustration is a different development task from a new insert, opening or compartment layout. Tell the supplier which parts are already approved. Even when the structure stays the same, print, materials, quantity and available production capacity still need confirmation.

Request a written list of inclusions: outer box, insert, liner, individual wrap, ribbon, labels, shipping outer and assembly. Product photographs do not establish what is supplied. Do not assume an existing format is in stock or that a simpler design automatically meets your date.

Fit the contents before choosing the seasonal print

Send measurements in a stated unit and distinguish usable internal space from the external box size. Include wrappers, caps, decorations and the filled weight. Review a representative loaded sample, not just an empty shell or a rendered image. The formats below are design options to review, not a claim of ready stock or tested shipping performance.

Keep food contact and shipping protection separate

For cookie gifts, identify the layer that actually touches the food. State the food type and intended packing, storage and contact conditions. FDA's US reference distinguishes food types and conditions of use for food-contact substances; the European Commission provides a separate framework for food-contact materials in the EU. Use the appropriate destination-market requirements to request documentation for the final components, not a generic 'food-safe' promise.

Decorative board, a printed image or a general certificate is not proof that every illustrated liner or wrap suits your recipe. This guide does not establish shelf life, allergen separation or certification of an MXPACKPRO product. Have the relevant food and packaging requirements reviewed for the actual application.

A presentation box and a parcel shipping pack are also separate specifications. Ask which cushioning, shipping outer and packed-sample review are needed for your contents and delivery route. A paper ornament cradle, drawer grid or small favor closure is not a published courier-protection rating.

Send a quotation brief that does not leave the hard parts out

  1. Contents and layout

    List the items in each pack, their dimensions and filled weight. Attach a drawing or sample where the cap, wrapper, coating or shape affects fit.

  2. Quantity per version

    Separate quantities by size, artwork and language. Ask for the applicable MOQ for each specification; do not apply one product's starting quantity to another.

  3. Exact supply scope

    Identify the box, insert, food-facing components, accessories and shipping outer you need supplied. State who will assemble and fill them.

  4. Artwork and approval

    State whether the dieline, print file and color references are ready. Ask which structural and printed samples are proposed and what approval each one covers.

  5. Destination and receiving date

    Give the filling location, delivery postcode and required packaging-in-hand date. Keep customer dispatch and final gift-delivery dates separate.

  6. Comparable commercial terms

    Ask the quotation to identify sample charges, tooling or setup, packaging, freight and relevant delivery responsibilities. Confirm scope and schedule in writing before treating different quotes as equivalent.

If the proposed schedule does not fit, simplify before you commit

Ask which changes can be reviewed without changing the approved fit: fewer artwork versions, a different finish, or a simpler opening may be worth comparing. If you consider a locally available temporary pack, confirm its availability, decoration and intended use independently. These are alternatives to investigate, not services or delivery dates promised by MXPACKPRO.

Do not recover time by skipping the checks that define the pack. Reconfirm the sample, included components, arrival assumptions and approved version after any change. Keep a dated approval record so the seasonal artwork is not confused with a different structure on a repeat order.

Purchasing questions

What is the last order date for custom Christmas packaging?

There is no single date that applies to every product or destination. Send the required empty-pack arrival date, postcode, quantity, artwork status and sample needs. Sampling, approval, production and delivery must be confirmed for that exact project before an order date can be proposed.

Is changing Christmas artwork always faster than making a new box?

Not automatically. Reusing an approved structure can remove some structural decisions, but print approvals, materials, quantity and production capacity still need review. Ask which stages remain and obtain a project-specific schedule.

Can a divided drawer box keep different cookies fresh or allergen-free?

A divider organizes the layout; it does not establish freshness, shelf life or allergen separation. Specify the actual food-facing liners or individual wraps and have the packed food application reviewed. No such performance is established by the design illustration.

Is an ornament gift carton ready to send by courier?

Do not assume so. Confirm the ornament's sphere, cap, complete height, weight and surface sensitivity, then review the cradle, cushioning and shipping outer on a packed sample. No courier-protection rating is published for the illustrated design.

Are the pictured Christmas boxes in stock, and do they have one MOQ?

The illustrated formats are design options, not evidence of inventory. There is no single MOQ across them. Availability, minimum quantity, materials, sample route and timing require confirmation for the chosen specification.
