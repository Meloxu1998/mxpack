Pyramid Catering Box Size and Sample Guide

Plan each pyramid catering-box tray, food height and serving sequence. Compare sample checks and prepare a brief for custom afternoon-tea packaging.

MXPACKPRO team · Published 2026-11-05

Size a pyramid catering box from the usable space on every tray, the height of the food and the way the layers will be removed. The pointed outer cover is only one part of the specification. Start with a menu layout and confirm the complete arrangement on a physical sample before approving production.

Separate the two pyramid-box uses

A tiered catering box presents a sandwich, pastry or afternoon-tea assortment on graduated trays beneath a pointed cover. A small pyramid favor box holds a compact gift. These share a silhouette, not necessarily their construction, usable capacity or assembly method.

The MXPACKPRO catering series groups three supplier reference views into one product page. The mixed-food and bakery views illustrate different arrangements. A reference with 15, 20 and 25 cm tray labels does not establish usable internal dimensions, depth or finished production sizes. Confirm each of those measurements on the approved drawing and sample.

Plan serving and transport together

Decide whether the buyer will uncover all layers at once, remove them one by one or transfer the contents to a display. Show the supplier the desired sequence. Confirm grip areas, support pieces, the outer closure and any separate carrier before finalizing artwork.

Use a physical prototype with representative sandwiches, pastries, liners and closed condiment pots. Check assembly, cover clearance, the loaded carrying arrangement and tray removal after the intended delivery journey. Treat the outcome as an observation for that tested configuration, not a general load rating or shelf-life claim.

Confirm the food-facing components

Ask which trays, liners, wraps or cups will touch each food. Describe the foods and their intended packing, storage and contact conditions. Keep wet condiments and delicate decorations in the layout review rather than assuming that decorative board provides the necessary barrier.

FDA's US reference distinguishes food types and conditions of use for food-contact substances. Documentation should match the final components and application in the destination market. A general certificate or a reference photograph does not establish suitability for every menu.

Approve structure before print

  1. Send the portion layout

    List each tray's menu, portion dimensions, food height, liners, cup sizes and filled weight. Include the preferred layer count and how the box will be served.

  2. Check an unprinted structural sample

    Review support, stacking sequence, cover clearance and tray removal. Record any changes to usable dimensions before committing to print areas.

  3. Confirm the whole order

    Approve material, food-contact components, artwork versions, quantity, packing configuration and destination together. Request a confirmed sample and delivery schedule against your required arrival date.

Prepare a destination-specific quotation

For a North American or Middle Eastern project, name the destination country and city rather than requesting one regional shipping price. Include the delivery postcode where used, whether the address is commercial or residential, quantity per design and the required arrival date. Ask which delivery stages and charges the quotation includes; a shipping estimate is not a guaranteed arrival date.

Choose one primary dimension unit, such as millimetres or inches, and label every tray measurement as internal or external. If you provide a second unit, identify it as a conversion rather than a different size. Confirm whether the cover, trays, supports and liners are included, how the pack is supplied for assembly, and the proposed export-carton quantity and dimensions. These are project questions, not published stock specifications.

When a flat grazing box is the better comparison

Compare a divided grazing box when a single-layer layout or direct access to all portions matters more than the tiered reveal. Compare the complete order: food clearance, carrying arrangement, assembly steps, storage configuration and quoted cost. Neither format has a universally better capacity or transport performance without reviewing the packed specification.

Also decide whether guests share the assortment at one serving time or collect individual orders at different times. Walk through portion access, assembly and tray removal with the actual menu. Headcount alone does not establish which format fits your service.

Purchasing questions

Are 15, 20 and 25 cm the usable internal tray sizes?

They are labels visible in one supplier reference, not confirmed usable dimensions. Ask for internal length, width, depth, clearance beneath the cover and a prototype with your actual assortment.

Does the pyramid catering box always have three trays?

Do not treat the reference layout as a fixed specification. Confirm the intended layer count, support arrangement and final dimensions for your menu before approving a sample.

Can I use a small pyramid gift box for afternoon-tea catering?

Do not assume the gift format can accommodate the menu. It is a different construction. Specify usable tray area, food height, weight and serving method when selecting a catering format.

What should I send for a useful pyramid-box quotation?

Send a per-tray menu layout, usable dimensions, food and cup heights, filled weights, liner requirements, quantity per design, artwork status, destination and required arrival date. MOQ and timing are confirmed for the final specification.

Reference

FDA: Food Types and Conditions of Use for Food Contact Substances

US food-contact reference; not MXPACKPRO certification or evidence of compliance in Canada or a Middle Eastern market. Confirm the destination-market requirements for the final pack. Checked 2026-11-05.
