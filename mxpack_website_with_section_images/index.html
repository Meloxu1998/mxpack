Ordering questions

All FAQs
Which food packaging products can I order?

Compare takeout boxes, burger boxes, pizza cartons, paper cups and carriers, paper bags, and bakery packaging. The full catalog also includes BBQ packaging, catering, retail and gift formats.

View all 41 formats
What is the minimum order quantity?

The current catalog lists starting quantities of 250 or 300 or 500 pieces by format. Final MOQ is confirmed for each size, material and print design, rather than the combined order total.

Understand MOQ & pricing
Can I customize the size and printing?

Share the packed product dimensions, required quantity and artwork if available. We review the structure, material, print area and dieline before confirming a specification.

Review custom packaging
Can I review a sample before production?

Ask for a structural or printed sample for your proposed specification. Sample scope, charges and timing are confirmed with the project; test the packed product before approving production.

Plan sampling
What should I send for a quotation?

Send the packaging type, internal size, quantity per design, food or product, delivery country and target arrival date. Include artwork if it is ready, and mark undecided details clearly.

Send your packaging brief
Which materials and food-contact documents should I request?

Specify the food type, packing temperature, contact time and destination market. Request documents for the exact material and use conditions; a general certificate does not establish suitability for every product.

Check material requirements
How long do production and shipping take?

Timing is confirmed after the structure, quantity, print and sample approval requirements are known. Share your destination postcode and required arrival date so production and freight can be reviewed together.
