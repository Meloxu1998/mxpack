Buyer checklist

Check fit before you commit.

  • Tell us the refrigeration and handoff conditions so moisture exposure can be reviewed.
  • Confirm which surfaces contact food and whether you will use an inner tray or liner.
  • Check that sauce containers remain upright and the closed lid clears the food.

Purchasing questions

Ordering printed sushi meal box.

Can this sushi box go in a microwave?

Microwave suitability is not assumed from the image or product name. Confirm the exact board, coating, printing, insert and intended heating conditions before making any heating claim.

What is the starting order quantity for printed sushi meal box?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send packed sushi layout, tray dimensions, sauce cups and finished height, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
