Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Can I use these bags for warm bread?

Tell us the bread temperature when packed and how long it stays in the bag. Review the paper and any lining for the actual recipe, then test moisture buildup, texture and handling with a sample before approving production.

What is the starting order quantity for printed baguette & bread bag?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send maximum loaf width, loaf length and the coverage you want, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
