Buyer checklist

Before you order

  1. Allow space above the bun so closing the lid does not press the toppings.
  2. Share how long the burger stays packed and whether fries travel in the same order.
  3. Ask for a sample closure test with your actual burger before approving the print run.

Quotation brief

Your quotation brief

For a burger-box quotation, send the finished burger diameter and height, wrapping dimensions, quantity per size or artwork, and delivery country. Add a photo of the assembled order and explain whether fries travel separately. Ask us to confirm internal dimensions, closure and sample scope in the same quotation.

Your specification brief

Build your burger-box specification.

Start with the order as the customer receives it. Use the same packed burger to compare proposed box dimensions and a structural sample.

Project detailInclude in your brief
FootprintMeasure the finished burger at its widest point, including any wrap that remains inside the carton. Request internal, not just external, box dimensions.
Lid clearanceInclude toppings and the highest point of the bun. Check that the proposed closed lid does not press the food during your packing trial.

Artwork versionsState the pieces required for each size and design. Review artwork against the agreed dieline rather than transferring it from a differently sized lid.

Destination and pack-out

Confirm the burger-box pack-out.

For a North American or Middle Eastern restaurant enquiry, compare the complete delivered scope as well as the printed carton. Use one approved size and artwork breakdown throughout the brief.

  • Label the internal length, width and closed height in millimetres or inches. If you supply both units, identify the primary measurement and the conversion. Approve the matching dieline and packed-burger sample rather than assuming a regional standard size.
  • List pieces for each box size and artwork version. Confirm how the cartons are supplied for assembly, pieces per export carton and the proposed outer-carton dimensions and weight. Do not infer a shipping configuration from an assembled product photo.
  • Give the destination country, city and postcode where used, plus the required arrival date. Ask which freight, clearance and destination charges are included, and request separate sample, production and transit estimates. The written quotation confirms the scope; no delivery date or market approval is implied.

Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

How do I choose the right burger box size?

Measure the finished burger rather than the bun alone. Include toppings, wrapping and the clearance needed to close the lid. A structural sample lets you check fit before confirming production.

What is the starting order quantity for printed burger clamshell box?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send finished burger diameter and height, including toppings, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

Can one clamshell size cover burgers with different topping heights?

Provide the smallest and largest finished burgers, with their normal wrapping. Review clearance and movement on a structural sample for both orders. A single size may be worth comparing, but fit is not established by bun diameter or a picture of the box.

Should a burger clamshell hold the fries as well?

Specify the complete meal layout before requesting a price. This page concerns the hinged burger carton; a meal carton or separate fries pack is a different layout. Confirm which components are included and test the assembled order before approving dimensions.
