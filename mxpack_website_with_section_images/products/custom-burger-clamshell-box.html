Buyer checklist

Before you order

  1. Allow space above the bun so closing the lid does not press the toppings.
  2. Share how long the burger stays packed and whether fries travel in the same order.
  3. Ask for a sample closure test with your actual burger before approving the print run.

Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

How do I choose the right burger box size?

Measure the finished burger rather than the bun alone. Include toppings, wrapping and the clearance needed to close the lid. A structural sample lets you check fit before confirming production.

What is the starting order quantity for printed burger clamshell box?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send finished burger diameter and height, including toppings, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
