Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Can a printed mailer ship without an outer carton?

That depends on the product, carrier requirements and the protection provided by the complete pack. Review transit testing and the need for an outer carton with your actual product rather than assuming the printed mailer is sufficient.

What is the starting order quantity for printed corrugated mailer?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send product dimensions, protective inserts, packed weight and required shipping carton, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
