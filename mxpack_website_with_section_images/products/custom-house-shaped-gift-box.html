Buyer checklist

Before you order

  1. Measure usable internal space below the sloping roof, not just the external peak height.
  2. Check roof movement, retention and access to every item with the complete gift arrangement on a structural sample.
  3. Confirm the supplied components. If food is included in your own assortment, review primary wraps or liners separately; decorative board does not establish food-contact suitability.

Quotation brief

Your quotation brief

Send a dimensioned gift layout, tallest item, filled mass, quantity per artwork and delivery country. Describe the required roof opening and any insert, individual packs or ribbon. Ask for the usable internal dimensions and a structural sample of the complete proposed construction. Confirm print files, component scope, applicable MOQ and schedule in the quotation.

Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Is this the same as a gable-top takeaway meal box?

No. This reference is a house-shaped presentation pack whose roof cover opens over a gift compartment. The existing gable meal carton uses another folding and carrying arrangement for takeaway orders. Do not transfer its handle, assembly, dimensions or food-use specification to this gift box.

What is the starting order quantity for house-shaped gift box with opening roof?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send packed gift footprint, maximum product height, usable space below the roof, insert layout and clearance for opening the cover, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

How should the gift height be measured under a pitched roof?

Provide each item's maximum packed height and its position in the proposed layout. Request usable internal height at those positions, including insert and cover clearance. External peak height alone does not describe the space available to the products.

Does the house-shaped box include an advent calendar or food compartments?

Not by default. This page describes the opening-roof presentation body shown in the reference. Numbered compartments, individual cartons, dividers, food wraps and contents are separate requirements that must be identified and confirmed in the quotation.
