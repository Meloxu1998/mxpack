Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

How is this different from a macaron sleeve or a window bakery carton?

This is a broad rigid sleeve-and-drawer construction with a grid for a mixed assortment. The macaron format is a narrow linear sleeve and tray, while window bakery cartons use other opening structures. Choose the format by the portion layout, packing method and sample checks rather than the seasonal artwork.

What is the starting order quantity for cookie assortment drawer box?

There is no fixed published MOQ for this design. Send the dimensions, quantity, insert requirements and finish. Availability, minimum order and sample route are confirmed after the construction review; do not use another product's MOQ as a promise for this format.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send individual portion dimensions, compartment layout and depth, liner allowance, drawer travel and packed assortment weight, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

Can unwrapped cookies be placed directly in the box?

Do not assume that the printed wrap, rigid board or illustrated liners are suitable for direct food contact. Specify the cookie type, fat content, storage conditions and destination market so the actual liners, wraps and supporting documentation can be confirmed.

Can Christmas artwork be changed without creating a new box design?

Artwork can be discussed for the same approved structure. A new color or seasonal print is not a separate structural product. Dimensions, divider layout, print process, MOQ and schedule still require project confirmation.
