Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Can I use the lids I already buy?

Provide the lid model and measured brim dimensions, or send a sample. Similar published cup capacities do not guarantee the same lid fit. Confirm compatibility before placing a production order.

What is the starting order quantity for printed coffee paper cup?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send required capacity, brim diameter, cup height and the lid reference, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
