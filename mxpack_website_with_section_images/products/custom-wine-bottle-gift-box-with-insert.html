Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Can this presentation box be used alone as a shipping carton?

Do not assume so. The fitted insert and gift shell must be reviewed with the filled bottle and the intended handling route. A separate protective shipping carton and suitable transit testing may be needed. No drop-protection rating is published for this design.

What is the starting order quantity for wine bottle gift box with fitted insert?

There is no fixed published MOQ for this design. Send the dimensions, quantity, insert requirements and finish. Availability, minimum order and sample route are confirmed after the construction review; do not use another product's MOQ as a promise for this format.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send bottle height, maximum diameter, neck and shoulder profile, full weight and insert clearance, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.
