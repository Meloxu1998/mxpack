Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Is this a folding advent carton with perforated doors?

No. This design is a rigid cabinet with two larger front doors and separate pull-out drawers. The existing folding advent carton uses a different access-panel structure. Drawer count, component dimensions and closure details are project-specific and require a sample review.

What is the starting order quantity for double-door drawer advent calendar box?

There is no fixed published MOQ for this design. Send the dimensions, quantity, insert requirements and finish. Availability, minimum order and sample route are confirmed after the construction review; do not use another product's MOQ as a promise for this format.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send drawer count, internal dimensions per drawer, item weights, full cabinet dimensions and door clearance, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

Must every advent-calendar drawer have the same internal size?

Do not assume equal drawers or a fixed drawer count from the illustrative image. List each gift, its dimensions, weight and removal clearance. Use that item schedule to review the drawer layout, cabinet dimensions and structural prototype. Feasibility, minimum quantity and sampling are confirmed for the proposed construction.

What should I confirm before ordering a custom advent-calendar cabinet?

Confirm the contents schedule, drawer extraction, door clearance, insert fit, artwork numbering and protection of the loaded cabinet. Work backwards from the required arrival date to sample approval, production and delivery, with each timing confirmed in the quotation. No Christmas arrival deadline or production lead time is guaranteed on this page.
