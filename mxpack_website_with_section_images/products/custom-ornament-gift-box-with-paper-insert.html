Buyer checklist

Before you order

  1. Measure the hanging cap and ribbon as well as the sphere; the insert opening must not force fragile parts.
  2. Test loading and removal with the actual ornament and review abrasion at paper contact points.
  3. A gift carton is not a tested postal shipper. Review cushioning, an outer carton and transport testing separately; the pictured bauble is not included.

Quotation brief

Your quotation brief

Send your measurements, estimated quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Include the sample checks above and your target date. Pricing is based on the complete specification, not the example image alone.

Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

Does the box include the bauble, and is it ready for courier shipping?

The bauble is shown only to explain the insert fit and is not included. No courier protection or drop-test rating is published. Review the filled carton, paper cradle, cushioning and shipping outer with the intended delivery route before approving the pack.

What is the starting order quantity for ornament gift box with paper insert?

There is no fixed published MOQ for this design. Send the dimensions, quantity, insert requirements and finish. Availability, minimum order and sample route are confirmed after the construction review; do not use another product's MOQ as a promise for this format.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send sphere diameter, overall height including cap, cap width, ribbon allowance, ornament weight and internal carton clearance, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

What do you need to size the ornament insert?

Send a drawing or sample showing the sphere diameter, hanging cap, full height and weight. Include surface sensitivity and filling method. The cradle aperture, contact points and box clearance are confirmed on a prototype, not from a decorative image alone.
