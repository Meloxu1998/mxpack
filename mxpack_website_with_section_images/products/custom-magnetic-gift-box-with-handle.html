Buyer checklist

Before you order

  1. Check usable internal space with the complete assortment and any insert, rather than the outside case dimensions alone.
  2. Review handle position, attachment and reinforcement using the actual filled pack; no carrying-weight rating is inferred from the photograph.
  3. Approve the closure, opening sequence and packing configuration together. Confirm whether the quotation includes the insert, handle and any decorative accessories.

Quotation brief

Your quotation brief

Send the complete gift dimensions, a layout photo or drawing, filled mass, required quantity per artwork and delivery country. Specify the insert and handle requirements and ask for a sample of the proposed closure and attachment. Confirm assembled or flat delivery and outer-carton packing in the quotation; contents and decorative items in the reference image are not included by default.

Purchasing questions

Ordering questions

How is this different from a gift bag or a standard magnetic gift box?

This brief combines an enclosed presentation case, magnetic closure and an attached carry handle. A gift bag uses a different body and handle arrangement, while the existing standard magnetic box is not presented as an integrated handled case. Review internal fit, handle attachment and filled-pack handling on this specific construction rather than transferring another format's specification.

What is the starting order quantity for magnetic gift box with carry handle?

This format has a starting order of 250 pieces for this format. The final minimum depends on the approved size, board, print process and packing arrangement. Your project quotation confirms the applicable quantity.

What should I send for an accurate quotation?

Send complete gift length, width and height, proposed insert layout, total filled mass and required handle clearance, the order quantity, artwork status and delivery country. Tell us the intended use and target date so the sample route, board specification and production schedule can be reviewed.

Can I check a sample before production?

Ask about the available stock sample or a custom structural prototype. A sample should be checked with your actual product before approving the dieline and print. Sample charges, shipping and timing are confirmed in the quotation.

Is there a published safe carrying weight?

No carrying-weight rating is published here. Provide the complete filled mass and intended handoff so the proposed board, handle and reinforcement can be reviewed. Check a fully packed sample before approving the construction; the image is not a load test.

Can the Christmas print be replaced with our branding?

Custom artwork can be reviewed for the same handled-case brief. Provide your logo, colors, finish locations and quantity per design. The approved dieline, sample, applicable MOQ and schedule are confirmed for that specification; a new print is not another structural product.
