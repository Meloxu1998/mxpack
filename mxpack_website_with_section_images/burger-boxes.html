Choose the order format

A burger box or a complete meal pack?

FormatWhat to confirm
Printed single-burger clamshellFor one finished burger: measure the widest point, full topping height and wrapping. Check the tuck closure on the packed sample.
Mini burger sharing cartonFor repeated portions in a sharing layout: arrange the actual mini burgers and confirm the insert positions and covered height.
Gable-top meal cartonFor a broader meal brief: list every item in the order and identify any separate wrapping or container positions that need review.

Prepare your quotation

Send a usable burger-packaging brief.

Include the assembled burger dimensions, wrapping, quantity per size and artwork, delivery country and required arrival date. Show the complete order and flag any proposed fries, drink or sharing layout.

Ask for internal dimensions, sample scope and the exact components included in the quotation. Food-contact documentation and any barrier proposal are reviewed for the intended conditions, not inferred from the artwork.

Discuss the order format

Destination and pack-out

Compare destination-ready quotations, not lid photographs.

For a United States, Canadian, UAE, Saudi Arabian or other destination brief, identify the actual delivery country and city. North America and the Middle East are not single shipping or documentation specifications.

  • Choose the order format first: single burger, sharing assortment or a complete meal. Record internal dimensions with a stated unit and pieces required for each size and artwork. Keep the same brief when comparing suppliers.
  • Ask which cartons, wraps, inserts and any separate meal components are included. Confirm the proposed assembly and export packing, carton quantity, dimensions and packed weight; these are not established by the example artwork.
  • Provide the postcode where used and required arrival date. Request separate sample, production and transport estimates and a clear list of included freight, clearance and destination charges. Describe the actual food-contact conditions and request documentation for the final specification, not a blanket regional certification.

Frequently asked questions

Ordering burger boxes.

What is the minimum order quantity for burger boxes?

The minimum is confirmed for the selected structure, finished size, artwork and packing arrangement. The individual product page shows the starting quantity for that format where verified; do not apply one product's minimum to every burger-box design.

Which burger dimensions should I send?

Measure the widest point and full height of the assembled burger, including toppings and the wrapping that stays inside. Label millimetres or inches and confirm whether each proposed dimension is internal or external. If both units are supplied, identify the primary measurement. Check lid clearance on a structural sample rather than assuming a standard burger-box size.

Should I order one box for a burger, fries and a drink?

List the complete order before choosing a format. Compare a single-burger clamshell for the burger and a separate meal or container layout when additional items need positions. Do not assume a single-burger carton includes compartments or drink support.

Can I print my own logo and artwork?

Provide the logo file, print colors and quantity for each artwork version. Review the design on the agreed size-specific dieline and confirm the proposed print process and finish in the quotation.

How should I check a sample before production?

Ask which sample route is available for the proposed construction. Use the actual finished burger and wrapping to review clearance, closure and your packing workflow. Sample scope, charges, shipping and timing are confirmed for the project.

How are materials and delivery timing confirmed?

Describe the recipe, packing temperature, contact time and destination market for material and documentation review. Provide the required arrival date and artwork status. The quotation confirms the proposed sampling, production and transport plan; this page does not guarantee a holding time or delivery date.
