Catalog questions

What buyers usually ask first.

These answers are starting points. Your quotation confirms the actual structure, MOQ, schedule and price.

What food packaging products can MXPACKPRO customize?

We support takeaway cartons, burger boxes, pizza boxes, paper cups, paper carrier bags, bakery boxes and coordinated custom packaging families.

What is the minimum order quantity?

The listed formats start from 250 or 300 pieces, depending on the product. The applicable MOQ is confirmed for your structure, material, size and printing method.

Can I order samples before production?

Yes. Stock samples and custom prototypes are available depending on the structure and project stage.

What information is needed for a packaging quote?

Share the food type, dimensions or capacity, estimated quantity, artwork status, delivery country and target date. These details help us recommend the right structure and next step.
