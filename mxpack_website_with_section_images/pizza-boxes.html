Frequently asked questions

Ordering pizza boxes.

How do I specify pizza box dimensions?

Measure the finished pizza diameter and height, including toppings, liners or a tray. State the required internal footprint and lid clearance. Confirm fit and closure using a packed sample rather than relying only on a menu size.

Can I combine several pizza sizes in one order?

Provide a separate quantity for each size and artwork version. The applicable MOQ, setup requirements and quotation are confirmed for each specification; do not assume the combined quantity qualifies every version.

Should I order the same box for collection and delivery?

Describe both service journeys, including holding time, handling and packing conditions. Review the complete pack with your actual product before deciding whether one specification suits both uses. No fixed heat-retention or delivery-performance claim is assumed.

What information helps compare printed pizza box quotations?

Send internal dimensions, quantities by artwork version, material requirements, the delivery country or postcode, required arrival date and artwork status. Request a quotation against the same brief so box, print and shipping assumptions can be compared.

Where can I check the current pizza carton structure?

The printed corrugated pizza box product page shows the current flat-folding carton with a hinged lid. Use that page for its product-specific starting details and this category checklist for planning menu sizes, artwork versions and sampling.
