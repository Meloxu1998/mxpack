Before you order

Make the seasonal brief practical.

  1. Send packed-product dimensions and a drawing or sample. Include any ornament cap, cake board, liner or bottle insert.
  2. State the quantity per size and artwork, the required arrival date, destination country and postal code.
  3. Approve the structure and print before production. Review direct-contact layers and shipping protection separately.

Choose the right route

Artwork update or new construction?

An existing format with new festive artwork can be considered separately from a new construction. A custom insert, shaped closure or drawer layout needs a structural review. Advent calendars also need time for compartment planning, filling and assembly.

Christmas Packaging FAQ

Can I still order custom packaging for this Christmas?

Send the required arrival date, quantity, artwork status, delivery country and postal code first. Sampling, approval, production, packing and shipping must be reviewed together. An advent campaign may need its packaging before December begins. We do not publish a guaranteed Christmas arrival date; availability and timing are confirmed for the specific project.

Can I add Christmas artwork to an existing packaging format?

Start with the format that fits your contents, then send the festive artwork for print and dieline review. Changing a color or illustration does not necessarily require a different construction. A new insert, closure or compartment layout requires a structural review, and the final print process, MOQ and timing remain project-specific.

What is the minimum order for Christmas packaging?

There is no single seasonal MOQ. A starting quantity shown on an existing product applies only to that format and remains subject to specification. New design options have no fixed published MOQ; size, construction, insert, print and quantity must be reviewed in a project quotation.

Are gifts, ornaments or food included?

No. These listings concern packaging formats. Any shown contents illustrate fit and are not included. Food-contact layers, filled-pack handling and shipping protection require separate confirmation for the actual contents and market.

Are all of the product images photographs?

No. Cards marked Design visualization are original illustrative designs, not photographs of produced samples or proof of stock. Other cards show format references. Final artwork, size, construction and availability must be confirmed on the project brief and sample.
