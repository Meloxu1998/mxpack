Cost planning

What Determines the Price of Custom Food Packaging?

Understand how size, structure, material, print coverage and quantity affect price.

Practical purchasing guidance

Check the selected product for its listed starting MOQ. Final MOQ and price depend on the approved size, material, printing and quantity per design; they are confirmed in the quotation.

Compare like-for-like quotations

Quotation itemInformation to provideWhy it changes the comparison
Format & sizeInternal dimensions, packed weight and any insert or liner.Material consumption and construction differ by specification.
Order quantityUnits per size and artwork version; repeat-order plan if known.A combined total may not satisfy the MOQ for each version.
Artwork & finishingPrint coverage, number of versions and requested finishes.Setup, tooling and finishing may be quoted separately.
Samples & approvalStructural or printed sample; delivery destination.Sample charges, revisions and approval timing need a clear scope.
Packing & deliveryDestination postcode, delivery term and required arrival date.Freight, duties and final delivery are not automatically included in the unit price.

A useful quotation brief

  1. Start with the essentials

    Share the format, internal size, quantity per design, food or product, destination and target arrival date. Attach artwork later if it is not ready.

  2. Separate confirmed from estimated

    Mark undecided items clearly. Ask for suitable alternatives instead of comparing offers based on different assumptions.

  3. Confirm the total scope

    Check sample and tooling charges, production timing after approval, packing, payment terms and the delivery term before ordering.
