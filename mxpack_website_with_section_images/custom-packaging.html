Before you approve

Questions worth asking.

Sample scope, costs and timing depend on the proposed specification. Check the details for your project rather than assuming a standard promise applies.

Packaging buying guide
What can I customize?

Discuss the structure, internal dimensions, inserts, material and artwork for your selected format. The available construction and print options are confirmed against your packed product, quantity and intended use.

Is the same minimum quantity available for every format?

No. MOQ is confirmed per structure, size, material and artwork version. Share quantities for each format separately so the quotation does not assume one combined minimum for an entire packaging range.

Can I check a sample before approving production?

Ask which stock, structural or printed sample is appropriate for your project. Sample availability, charges and timing require confirmation. Evaluate fit, closure, carrying and artwork against the agreed specification before approval.

What if my artwork or dimensions are not ready?

Send the product dimensions or a reference, the intended use, approximate quantity and destination. Mark undecided details clearly. The structure and artwork requirements can then be reviewed before a final quotation.

How are food-contact requirements and delivery timing confirmed?

For food packaging, specify the food type, packing temperature, contact time and destination market, then request documents for that exact specification. Production and delivery timing are reviewed after the specification, approval steps, quantity and destination are known.
